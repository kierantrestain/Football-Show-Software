# Fixture snapshot licence

The OpenLigaDB-derived fixture records in `footballSnapshot.generated.ts` and their API representation are adapted from [OpenLigaDB](https://www.openligadb.de/). They are offered under the [Open Database License 1.0](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/). This notice applies to the derived fixture database.

The adaptation normalises UTC kickoff timestamps, stable club identities, fixture keys, rounds and final scores, and removes image URLs. The PL, CL and EL upstream endpoint selection and independent checks are documented in the [football data evidence](../../docs/release/football-data.md). The code, club marks, photos and separately supplied studio materials have their own rights.
