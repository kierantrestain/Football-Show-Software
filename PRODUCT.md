# Touchline Studio

Touchline is a football production workstation for analysts, presenters, and broadcast operators. The opening production demonstrates five phases of a fictional match. The editing canvas, presenter controls, and television output are separate surfaces.

| Role | Primary job |
| --- | --- |
| Analyst | Load line-ups, stage formations, create movements, annotate footage, and prepare comparisons |
| Producer | Order scenes and cues, rehearse, take graphics to air, and control presenter access |
| Presenter | Advance or replay a cue, highlight a player, and manipulate a scene from a tablet |
| Viewer | Render the on-air feed on a television or OBS browser source |

Work starts in the sample editor. Studio Home manages saved productions, templates and versions. Match Data supplies line-ups and context. Graphics prepares broadcast-ready cards. Present is the control surface for rehearsal and air.

Positional reconstructions stay editable and carry provenance. The bundled clubs and most illustrative figures are fictional. Provider-backed records carry their source and unavailable metrics stay empty. The sample production uses no third-party badges or footage.
